Start with a little permission.

  1. 01

    Share a tab

    Choose a password, approve sharing, and select Share current tab. Share your GhostPair address and password with your guest.

  2. 02

    Connect with your host

    Your guest selects Connect to a host and enters those details. The approved tab appears in their browser.

  3. 03

    Browse together

    Preview interactions or switch to live control. Approve additional tabs individually, and end the session whenever you need.

You approve each shared tab and stay in control of the session. Pause sharing, withdraw control, or end the session at any time.

Visual only

Starts here

Clicks and typing are previews. Explore together without activating links, pressing page buttons, or changing original form values.

Live control

Host's choice

The host can switch modes to allow real page interactions, including basic clicks and text editing.

Scrolling, navigation, and tab management remain live in both modes.

What to know before connecting

Video and interactions travel directly over WebRTC. Some networks cannot establish a direct connection.

Clipboard text sync is optional and requires both participants. Browser tabs only; no audio or desktop sharing.

Get the latest release Chrome & Edge · Windows

  1. Choose your browser

    Download the Chrome or Edge ZIP from the release's Assets. Choose the extension package, rather than the source code archive.

  2. Extract the ZIP

    Keep the extracted folder in a permanent location on your computer.

  3. Load the extension

    Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the folder containing manifest.json.
