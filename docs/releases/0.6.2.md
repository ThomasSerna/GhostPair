# GhostPair 0.6.2

- Show the saved **Your connection code: {code}** in small text beneath **Share my tab** before starting a session. First-time installations explain that the code is generated when sharing.
- Default text and other previews to **For a short time**, initially 0.5 seconds, configurable from 0.1 to 10 seconds in steps of 0.1.
- Make button-press previews follow the selected duration, including **Until cleared**, alongside text and choice previews. Click circles and notices keep their brief lifetimes.
- Preserve saved duration choices, connection codes and identities; reduce saved temporary durations above 10 seconds to the new maximum. Permissions and peer protocol version 4 remain unchanged.
